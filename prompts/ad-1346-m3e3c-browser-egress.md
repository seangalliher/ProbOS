## 1. Problem

- `EGRESS_SITES` holds two `Pending("M3e3c", ...)` rows, `BrowserSession._start_impl` and `BrowserSession._connect_impl` (`tests/fixtures/ad1346_effect_census.py:202-204`); EN3 pins them (`tests/test_ad1346_admission_egress_census.py:114-121`).

## 2. Solution

- **Two checks.** `BrowserSession.start` and `BrowserSession.connect` each call `check_at(getattr(getattr(self, "_runtime", None), "admission_service", None), effect=EFFECT_EGRESS, presented_id=self._agent_id)` after the ended-session refusal and before `self._ensure_host()`.
- **The hand-off.** The bridge factory passes `runtime=self._runtime`, as `_create_session`'s does.
- **The census.** The two rows become `Sink("BrowserSession.start", "self._agent_id", via=("BrowserSession._start_and_record",))` and `Sink("BrowserSession.connect", "self._agent_id")`. The counts become 4 sink, 0 pending, 2 other-class, 28 system in 27 rows, 2 reached only from named files and 7 command-line in 5 rows: 43 points in 40 rows.
- **Nothing is refused.** `check_at` returns None and raises only what `check_ticket` raises, and the real `check_ticket` lets no `Exception` out (`admission/seam.py:51-55`; `admission/service.py:234-242`).
