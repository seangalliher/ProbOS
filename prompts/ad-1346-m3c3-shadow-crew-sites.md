## 2. Solution

- **Statements.** One `shadow_at(...)` statement after each of the six checks, before the site acts on the result, with no await between. Each carries `refused=<the check's record> is not None`.
  - At the two inline checks the check's call moves unchanged into a local.
  - At `_requester_stop` the returned call moves into `stopped`; the statement follows, then `return stopped`.
- **Reach.** Each site reaches the service the way it reaches its stop gate:
  - the delegation, both correction loops and the synthesis read it with `getattr` on the runtime they hold;
  - `CrewSessionFinalizer` and `AgentWorkService` take it as a constructor keyword beside their gate, from `startup/finalize.py` and `runtime.py`.
  - A None service, or any object that is not exactly an `AdmissionService`, is passed over by `shadow_at` (`seam.py:21-22`).
- **Requests.** Work kinds:
  - `delegated_task` at delegation (M3a's kind for that seam);
  - `agentic_run` at the four crew sites;
  - `agent_work`, appended to the vocabulary, at the create.
- **Projected correction.** `_session_correction_runtime` hands the service to its projected registry. The projected runtime gains no field.
- **Census.** The six rows move to `SHADOW_SITES` and `SHADOW_PENDING` becomes empty. 9b also pins each statement's `refused` expression.
- **T16.** The expected sets grow by `delegate_task_tool.py`, `crew_verifier.py`, `crew_synth.py`, `crew_finalizer.py` and `agent_work.py`. The router pin is unchanged.
- **G5** (one real boot): four of the six sites hand the boot's service today's outcome, and the store holds both rows for each driven (work kind, seam) pair.
- **G6** (two real boots, switch on and off): the calls G6 makes at the four sites give byte-identical answers.
