## 2. Solution

- **The checks (D314).** `ChannelManager.create_channel` calls `check_at(self._admission, effect=EFFECT_STORE_WRITE, presented_id=created_by)`, and `get_or_create_dm_channel` calls it with `presented_id=agent_a_id`. In each, the call is the statement before the `await self._db.execute(` of `INSERT INTO channels`. The module imports `check_at` and `EFFECT_STORE_WRITE` at module scope, as `ward_room/service.py:12-13` does.
- **The hand-off (D315).** `ChannelManager` gains a class-level `_admission: object = None` and an instance `set_admission`, which makes 13 direct methods (hand count). `WardRoomService.set_admission` calls it when `self._channels is not None`. That is 2 added lines, which takes `WardRoomService`'s body to 500 lines (hand count). `ProbOSRuntime.start` is not edited.
- **The rows (D313).** `ward_room/channels.py` leaves `STORE_WRITE_FILES` and `AGENT_ATTRIBUTED_SCOPES` and is split by scope in `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES`: 2 `StoreCheck` rows and 2 `System` rows.
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` then holds 14 checks and 46 `System` rows in 9 files (hand).
  - `STORE_WRITE_FILES` holds 70 rows: 3 pending, 66 the ship's, 1 the command line's (hand).
- **Shadow only.** `check_at` does nothing unless it is handed an exact `AdmissionService` (`admission/seam.py:57-61`). Nothing is refused, charged or answered differently (WM15).
