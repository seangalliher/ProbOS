## 2. Solution

- **The checks (D328).** `ArtifactStore.add_version` and `ArtifactStore.reconcile_exact_version` each call `check_at(self._admission, effect=EFFECT_STORE_WRITE, presented_id=created_by)` as the statement before their `with self._connect() as conn:`. The module imports `check_at` and `EFFECT_STORE_WRITE` at module scope.
- **The hand-off (D329).**
  - `ArtifactStore` holds `_admission: object = None` at class level, and `set_admission` sets the instance attribute.
  - `_notify_version_committed` becomes a module-level function that takes the callback as its first argument. Its two callers pass `self._version_committed_callback`. The class keeps 15 direct methods, and its body spans 348 lines (hand count).
  - `ProbOSRuntime.start` imports the class in the method and hands it the service under `isinstance`, after the assignment service's hand-off.
- **The rows (D327).** `artifacts/__init__.py` leaves `STORE_WRITE_FILES` and `AGENT_ATTRIBUTED_SCOPES`, and is split by scope in `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES`: 2 `StoreCheck` rows and 1 `System` row.
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` then holds 19 `StoreCheck` and 49 `System` rows in 11 files (hand count).
  - `STORE_WRITE_FILES` holds 68 rows: 1 pending, 66 the ship's, 1 the command line's (hand count).
- **Shadow only.** `check_at` does nothing unless it is handed an exact `AdmissionService` (`admission/seam.py:59-63`). Nothing is refused, charged or answered differently (WM19).

    each answer without the fields a fresh store draws, and the refusal's text."""
