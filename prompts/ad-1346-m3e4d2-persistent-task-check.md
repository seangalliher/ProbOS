## 2. Solution

- **The check (D292).** `create_task` gains `check_at(self._admission, effect=EFFECT_STORE_WRITE, presented_id=created_by)` as the statement before its `if self._db:`.
- **The hand-off (D293).** `PersistentTaskStore` gains a class-level `_admission: object = None` and an instance `set_admission`. `ProbOSRuntime.start` hands it `self.admission_service` (or None) under `isinstance`, after M3e4d1's hand-offs.
- **The rows (D291).** `persistent_tasks.py` leaves `STORE_WRITE_FILES` and `AGENT_ATTRIBUTED_SCOPES`, and is split by scope in `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES`: 1 `StoreCheck` row and 3 `System` rows.
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` then holds 9 checks and 18 `System` rows in 6 files.
  - `STORE_WRITE_FILES` holds 73 rows: 6 pending, 66 the ship's, 1 the command line's.
- **Shadow only.** `check_at` with anything but an exact `AdmissionService` does nothing (`admission/seam.py:54-58`). Nothing is refused, charged or answered differently (WM9).
