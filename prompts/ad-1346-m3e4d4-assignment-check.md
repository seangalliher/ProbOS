## 2. Solution

- **The check (D307).** `create_assignment` calls `check_at(self._admission, effect=EFFECT_STORE_WRITE, presented_id=created_by)` as the statement before its `await self._db.execute(` of `INSERT INTO assignments`. The module imports `check_at` and `EFFECT_STORE_WRITE` at module scope, as `persistent_tasks.py:22-23` does.
- **The hand-off (D308).** `AssignmentService` gains a class-level `_admission: object = None` and an instance `set_admission`: 15 direct methods (hand count). `ProbOSRuntime.start` hands it `self.admission_service` (or None) under `isinstance`, after the chat thread store's hand-off.
- **The rows (D306).** `assignment.py` leaves `STORE_WRITE_FILES` and `AGENT_ATTRIBUTED_SCOPES` and is split by scope in `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES`: 1 `StoreCheck` row and 4 `System` rows.
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` then holds 12 checks and 44 `System` rows in 8 files (hand).
  - `STORE_WRITE_FILES` holds 71 rows: 4 pending, 66 the ship's, 1 the command line's (hand).
- **Shadow only.** `check_at` does nothing unless it is handed an exact `AdmissionService` (`admission/seam.py:56-60`). Nothing is refused, charged or answered differently (WM13).
