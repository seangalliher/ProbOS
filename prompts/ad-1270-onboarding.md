## 1. Problem

The four ADs shipped "foundation cuts" (#439, #440, #441, #443, closed by `43691dbf`) that were never connected. No production code produces what they consume, so they cannot be wired without new design: a module-delivery and assessment producer (AD-507c, itself dormant) and a corrected module vocabulary. Their only hosts are inert. Keeping them on the list as `wire` overstates what exists; wiring them into an inert host would hide them.

## 2. Solution overview

Delete the module and its test. Regenerate the dormant baseline so its six rows leave. Record the retirement and what is lost in `DECISIONS.md`. Nothing else changes.
