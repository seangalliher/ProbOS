## 2. Solution

- **The pending-scope row (D334).**
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` may hold `Pending` values. `_SCOPE_PENDING = Pending("M3e4d", ...)` marks a scope that a later part classifies.
  - `workforce.py` leaves `STORE_WRITE_FILES` and `AGENT_ATTRIBUTED_SCOPES` (which becomes empty) and is split by scope: 1 `StoreCheck` row, 2 `System` rows and 45 `_SCOPE_PENDING` rows.
  - The 45 rows are generated from one tuple, `_WORKFORCE_PENDING`, so a later part removes a name and adds its row.
- **The check (D335).** `WorkItemStore._insert_work_item` calls `check_at(self._admission, effect=EFFECT_STORE_WRITE, presented_id=item.created_by)` as the statement before its `if self._db:`. The module imports `check_at` and `EFFECT_STORE_WRITE` at module scope.
- **The hand-off (D336).** `WorkItemStore.__init__` takes a last keyword, `admission: object = None`, and holds it as `self._admission`. `init_communication` passes the `admission` it is given.
- **Counts (hand count).**
  - `STORE_WRITE_SCOPES` holds 20 `StoreCheck`, 51 `System` and 45 `Pending` rows in 12 files.
  - `STORE_WRITE_FILES` holds 67 rows: 66 the ship's, 1 the command line's.
- **Shadow only.** `check_at` does nothing unless it is handed an exact `AdmissionService` (`admission/seam.py:60-64`). Nothing is refused, charged or answered differently (WM21).
